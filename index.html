About

A quick trace of the last 7 years

I'm a Data Scientist at IBM, where I've spent the last seven years working across the usual data science stack — from cleaning raw tables to shipping models that other teams actually rely on.

What keeps the job interesting is that the stack itself never sits still. Right now that means going deep on agentic AI: systems built from LLMs that can plan a task, call tools, check their own work, and follow through — rather than models that just return an answer and hand the rest back to a human.

This page is a mix of both worlds: a few classic predictive models from earlier work, and a set of newer projects where the system does more of the reasoning itself.
